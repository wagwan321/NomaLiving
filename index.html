Who can live at Noma?

Noma is designed for university students (LAU is right next door) but we also welcome interns and young professionals. Just tell us a bit about yourself when you apply.

Are bills included in the rent?

Yes. Electricity (24/7), water, Wi-Fi, air conditioning and heating are all included in your monthly rent. No generator subscriptions and no surprise bills.

Can I share a studio with a friend?

Absolutely. Our Twin Studios have two single beds and are priced per student, which makes them the most affordable option. Apply together, or ask us to match you with a roommate.

What's the minimum stay?

One month. Most students book a full semester or academic year, which comes with a discount (5% and 10%). For shorter stays, you can book directly on Booking.com.

Is there a deposit?

A refundable security deposit equal to one month's rent holds your studio. It's returned at the end of your stay after a quick check-out inspection.

Is cleaning included?

Your studio is cleaned before you arrive. During your stay, cleaning and laundry can be arranged on request through our team.

Can friends or family visit?

Of course, during the day. Parents visiting for a few nights can book a studio on Booking.com when one is free. We just ask that everyone respects quiet hours and the no-parties rule.

How do I pay?

Rent is paid monthly, per semester or per year, by card or bank transfer. You'll get a receipt for every payment.

Is the rooftop open?

Not yet. The shared rooftop is under maintenance and will open to residents soon. We'll let everyone know the moment it's ready.
